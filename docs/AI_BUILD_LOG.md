# AI Build Log

AI accelerated the work, but did not make the final product decisions.

## How AI was used

- **Market and competitive research:** mapped public Razorpay Buildathon repositories and compared ReCycle with Agent Studio, subscription recovery, abandoned-cart conversion and adjacent retention tools.
- **Adversarial validation:** compared ReCycle with a pre-dispute revenue-protection concept across data access, AI necessity, causal measurement, platform fit and one-day feasibility.
- **Specification:** translated the challenge rubric into explicit requirements for signal, decision, merchant control, learning, trust, failure handling and business outcome.
- **Construction:** generated and iterated the responsive Next.js experience, synthetic merchant cases, interaction states, deterministic policy gates and evaluation harness.
- **Communication:** compressed the product logic into a one-page note and 90-second narrative.

## Human decisions

- Chose ReCycle for its narrower merchant job, native payment-ledger signal and cleaner causal measurement.
- Focused the initial segment on single-category replenishable D2C brands.
- Made `DO_NOTHING` a first-class recommendation.
- Treated randomized holdout as a product feature, not an analytics afterthought.
- Kept rupee arithmetic, eligibility and policy limits outside the LLM.
- Removed unsupported market statistics.
- Excluded live messaging, automatic discounting and production-model claims.

## Human/AI boundary

AI proposes cadence interpretation, action uplift and editable message copy. Deterministic code checks identity, consent, order history, confidence, frequency, margin and discount limits. The merchant can approve, edit, snooze, exclude or stop. Real performance would require merchant data and a powered randomized experiment.

## Tools

- Claude: adversarial product evaluation.
- Gemini: early problem and market critique.
- Codex: research synthesis, product design, prototype implementation, harness and submission artifacts.
- Razorpay-style test flow: payment-link execution demonstration without moving money.

## Limitation

The prototype uses synthetic Sleepy Bean Coffee data. Probabilities, uplift and financial results demonstrate the decision experience; they are not production-performance claims.

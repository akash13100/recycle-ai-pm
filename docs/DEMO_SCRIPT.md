# 90-Second Demo Script

## Recording path

Opportunity Queue → Priya Menon → Approve & create test link → Open test checkout → Tara Iyer / Holdout → Learning Lab → Policies & safety.

## Narration

**0:00–0:12 — Opportunity Queue**

“A flat 30-day campaign mistimes every customer and discounts people who would have purchased anyway. ReCycle asks a better question: who will buy because the merchant acts?”

**0:12–0:27 — Queue and signal**

“ReCycle links captured payments by verified identity, learns each buyer’s purchase rhythm, and ranks drifting customers by expected incremental margin.”

**0:27–0:50 — Priya decision**

“Priya normally reorders every 28 to 36 days and is now at day 43. The model compares four actions. A one-tap link creates more expected margin than a discount. Code verifies consent, identity, frequency and margin limits; the merchant can edit, approve, snooze or stop.”

**0:50–0:59 — Test execution**

“Approval creates a Razorpay test link and an auditable decision. No real money moves.”

**0:59–1:10 — Holdout**

“Tara is randomly assigned to the protected holdout, so no intervention fires. This preserves the counterfactual.”

**1:10–1:24 — Learning Lab**

“The Learning Lab compares treatment with business as usual, measures incremental margin and opt-outs, and pauses strategies that fail to beat control.”

**1:24–1:30 — Close**

“Most retention tools predict who will buy. ReCycle predicts who will buy only if the merchant acts—and when doing nothing protects margin.”
